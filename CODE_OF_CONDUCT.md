# Code of Conduct

UniOS is a small project for students. We want everyone who takes part to feel safe and respected.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to code or to open source.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Accept that the maintainer makes the final decision on a change.
- Respect privacy. Do not post another person's data, timetable, attendance or contact details.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or threats.
- Comments that attack a person for their gender, caste, religion, region, language, disability, sexuality or background.
- Sexual language or images.
- Spam, or posting someone else's private information.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. It also applies when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@aashu035](https://github.com/aashu035)) can edit or remove comments, close issues, and block people who break this code.

The project has no private contact address yet. Until the maintainer adds one, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Do not put the details of a report in a public issue.
